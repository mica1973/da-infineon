Partner program’s mission

Our mission is to provide a profitable, comprehensive, and sustainable program that fosters mutual growth, empowered by our global resources and international support. Together, we can help our joint customers stay ahead of the curve and bring any idea to life.

Partner Ecosystems

The Partner Program comprises various ecosystems related to specific Infineon products or applications, organized into 3 growth areas – mobility, IoT, and energy efficiency. ​ ​ Each ecosystem provides various system and solution integrators, software and development tool experts, service providers, and module makers worldwide. This creates an environment with endless possibilities where customers can access all the necessary tools and support needed to develop advanced and comprehensive solution designs.
